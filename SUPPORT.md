# Support

## Community use

Public documentation and the repository issue tracker are the community support
channels for the open-source gateway. Issues should include a version or commit,
deployment environment, minimal reproduction, expected and actual behavior, and
sanitized diagnostics.

Community questions, issue submission, and private bug submission do not include
a human-response, incident-response, resolution, onboarding, or managed-operations
SLA. Never place a suspected vulnerability in a public issue; follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Verified Release Assurance

Organizations whose needs are met by the OSS feature set can purchase Verified
Release Assurance without buying enterprise fleet-management features. Release
Assurance is intended for teams that want a maintained, CISO-reviewable product
line with identified artifacts, release evidence, dependency notices, SBOMs,
vulnerability review, and supported update provenance.

Release Assurance does not include incident response, bespoke engineering,
managed operations, or guaranteed resolution unless those services are stated
in a separate signed agreement. For availability and terms, contact
<sales@routesphere.global>.

## Enterprise

Enterprise fleet management, multi-tenancy, organization-wide policy,
commercial Console capabilities, and separately contracted assistance are not
part of the open-source repository. Their scope and service terms are defined by
the applicable commercial agreement.
